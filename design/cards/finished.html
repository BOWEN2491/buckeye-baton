Club Sign-up Site

Alex R., Priya N. · Student org project · Spring 2025

A web form for student clubs to collect event sign-ups. Shipped and used by two clubs for fall recruiting. AI summary · confirmed by team

From the team

What we got

  • Cutting the form to 4 required fields raised completion from 3/8 to 7/8 testers.
  • A reusable mobile-first form component. View code
  • Testing with 8 first-year students caught problems our team never noticed.
