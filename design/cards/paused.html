Dining Hall Wait-time Tracker

Jordan T., Wei L. · Course project · Spring 2026

Tried to show live wait times at campus dining halls from student check-ins. Built a check-in flow and a campus map; paused before launch. AI summary · confirmed by team

Didn't ship, but the lessons did.

From the team

Why we paused

Not enough check-ins to be useful. We needed a steady stream every hour at each hall and got only a handful.

If we did it again

  • Talk to dining services first. They may already have card-swipe counts.
  • Pilot one dining hall, not six.
  • Check data volume before building the interface.
